# Legal Topic: Sale of Goods & Consumer Protection

## Passage R-05: Non-Delivery and Damaged Delivery
Source: Sale of Goods Act, Section 31 (PLACEHOLDER)
It is the duty of the seller to deliver the goods and of the buyer to accept and pay for them in accordance with the terms of the contract of sale. If a seller delivers fewer units than contracted for, or if goods arrive broken prior to handover, the buyer may reject the deficient portion and seek a pro-rata refund or replacement.

## Passage R-06: Burden of Proof and Evidence Weight
Source: Evidence Law in Civil Proceedings (PLACEHOLDER)
In civil disputes, the standard of proof is the balance of probabilities. Official third-party receipts, electronic bank transfer records, and contemporaneous written acknowledgements carry substantially higher probative weight than uncorroborated oral recollections or private undated notes prepared after a dispute arises.
