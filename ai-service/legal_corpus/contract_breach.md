# Legal Topic: Contract Formation & Breach

## Passage R-01: Performance and Payment Obligations
Source: Indian Contract Act, Section 37 (PLACEHOLDER)
When two parties enter into a valid contract for services or goods, each party is bound to perform their promised obligations unless such performance is excused under agreed terms. A party who has fulfilled agreed work is entitled to receive the contracted payment. If the counterparty withholds payment without proving a total breach or specified deficiency, the recipient is in default of payment obligations.

## Passage R-02: Partial or Defective Performance
Source: Principles of Civil Contract Law (PLACEHOLDER)
Where a contractor or service provider delivers defective or incomplete work, the client is entitled to deduct reasonable costs required to cure the defect or complete the work. However, the client is not entitled to retain the entire contracted amount where substantial performance has been completed and usable benefit has been retained.
