# Legal Topic: Rental Deposits and Tenancy Obligations

## Passage R-03: Security Deposit Refund & Deductions
Source: Model Tenancy and Rent Control Standards (PLACEHOLDER)
A landlord receiving a security deposit holds it in trust to guarantee the tenant performance of lease terms. Upon lawful surrender of the premises, the landlord is obligated to refund the deposit in full within a reasonable period, typically thirty days. Deductions are permissible solely for unpaid rent or proven tenant-caused damage exceeding ordinary wear and tear.

## Passage R-04: Burden of Proof on Property Damage
Source: Tenancy Civil Dispute Standards (PLACEHOLDER)
The burden of proving damage beyond normal wear and tear rests entirely on the party asserting the claim. A landlord claiming repair deductions must present contemporaneous documentation such as move-in/move-out inspection reports, photographs, and actual contractor repair invoices. In the absence of contemporaneous proof or itemized bills, the deposit must be returned.
